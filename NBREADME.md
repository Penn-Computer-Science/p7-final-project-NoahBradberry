Top Down Shooter

Premise: This is a basic top-down shooter game made with TKinter. It contains several enemy types and power-ups. The game consists of 30 waves that get harder as you go.

Enemy Types
Enemies spawn on one of the 4 sides of the screen and start moving towards the player. All enemies damage the player when they are next to the player.

1. Normal (Purple) - This is a normal enemy with nothing special about it. It dies from one hit.
2. Speedy (Pink) - This enemy is double the speed of a normal enemy and takes one hit to kill.
3. Tank (Red) - This enemy is slightly slower than a normal enemy and takes 3 hits to kill. As you damage it, it transitions from dark red to bright red.
4. Splitter (Green) - This enemy has the normal speed. When it is hit, this enemy turns into 2 light green enemies that take one hit and are normal speed.
5. Kamikaze (Orange) - When this enemy is hit, it makes a small blast and any enemies in this blast are killed. If the player is in the blast, it takes considerable damage from the blast.

Powerups
A powerup spawns every 15 seconds and moves around the map randomly until the player picks it up by colliding with it.

1. Spreadshot (Orange) - This powerup makes the player shoot 3 bullets instead of one for 5 seconds. The closer your mouse is to the player, the more the bullets are spread.
2. Health (Green) - This powerup simply gives the player back some health that they lost.
3. Bigger Bullets (Red) - This powerup doubles the size of the bullets for 5 seconds
4. Piercing Bullets (Purple) - This powerup makes the bullets able to go through the enemies and hit multiple enemies with one bullet.
